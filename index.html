How it works

From a thought to a trip

Four steps, one app — from "what if" to a plan in your pocket.

  1. 1

    Pick a place

    Type a city, or open the map and see where your passport takes you.

  2. 2

    We check the visa

    For your passport and where you live. When there's something to apply for, the official site is one tap away.

  3. 3

    We plan the days

    Real places, times that work, room to breathe. Every day is yours to tweak.

  4. 4

    Off you go

    Share a card with your crew, keep the PDF, book when you're ready.
