WHAT DECIDES YOUR ANSWER

🛂 Your passport

  • The main thing the check reads.The main thing brimm reads.
  • Answers differ from one passport to the next.

🏠 Where you live

  • Georgia counts a residence permit from a Gulf state — the UAE’s included.
  • It must be valid on the day you arrive.
  • 17 nationalities need more: a permit that allows more than one entry and a year left on it.

🎫 Visas you hold

  • A US, UK, Schengen or Canada visa you add is read with your passport.A US, UK, Schengen or Canada visa you hold can be part of it too.

🩺 Travel insurance is required for all tourists

  • Health and accident cover for every day of the trip.
  • It can be checked at the border.
