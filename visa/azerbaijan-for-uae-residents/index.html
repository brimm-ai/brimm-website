WHAT DECIDES YOUR ANSWER

🛂 Your passport

  • The main thing the check reads.
  • Answers differ from one passport to the next.

🏠 Your UAE residence

  • Azerbaijan counts a residence permit from a Gulf state — the UAE’s included.
  • It needs 6 months left on it when you arrive.

🎫 Visas you hold

  • A US, UK, Schengen or Canada visa you add is read with your passport.
