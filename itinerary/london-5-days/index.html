ITINERARY · UK · 5 DAYS

5 days in London: Westminster landmarks

London rewards the curious — where a Georgian townhouse pub sits beside a Brutalist arts centre and a 900-year-old fortress, all within a short walk.

🌤 Best Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct🛏 Stay: South Bank🚇 Metro + taxi💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVE AND EASE INTO THE SOUTH BANK

  • 14:00Heathrow airport → central London (~1h)
  • 16:00Check in · South Bank
  • 17:30Thames riverside walk, South BankFlat, lively, and the skyline does the work.
  • 19:30Dinner · around South Bank

DAY 2 · WESTMINSTER LANDMARKS

  • 10:00Westminster Abbey Book aheadThe Coronation Chair sits quietly behind the altar.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Westminster
  • 15:30Big Ben and Westminster BridgeCross the bridge for the best angle back.

DAY 3 · SOUTH KENSINGTON MUSEUMS AND HYDE PARK

  • 09:30Natural History MuseumStart with the main hall; the building is half the show.
  • 12:30Lunch · around South Kensington
  • 14:30Victoria and Albert MuseumGo for the jewellery and the Cast Courts.
  • 18:30Dinner · around South Kensington

DAY 4 · COVENT GARDEN AND SHOREDITCH

  • 10:00Covent Garden PiazzaStreet performers outside; the market hall is worth ducking into.
  • 12:30Lunch · around Covent Garden
  • 15:30Brick Lane and Shoreditch street artWalk the side streets, not just the main drag.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Shoreditch

DAY 5 · LAST MORNING AND DEPARTURE

  • 10:30Stroll through St James's ParkEasy, close to the centre, nothing to rush.
  • 12:00Central London → Heathrow airport (~1h)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Tap an Oyster or contactless card on every ride.
  • Many museums are free; book timed entry where offered.
  • Carry a light rain jacket year-round.
