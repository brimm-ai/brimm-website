5 days in Bangkok: Royal Bangkok Highlights

Bangkok is a city that never lets you catch your breath — one moment you're eating the best pad kra pao of your life from a street cart, the next you're inside a golden temple older than most nations.

🌤 Best Nov, Jan–Feb🚇 Metro + taxi💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND RIVERSIDE EVENING

  • 13:00Bangkok airport → Riverside (~1h)
  • 16:30Wat Arun at golden hourCross by the small ferry.
  • 18:30Dinner · around Riverside

DAY 2 · ROYAL BANGKOK HIGHLIGHTS

  • 08:30Grand Palace and Wat Phra KaewShoulders and knees covered.
  • 12:00Lunch · around Rattanakosin
  • 14:00Wat Pho Reclining BuddhaThe feet are inlaid with mother-of-pearl.
  • 18:30Dinner · around Banglamphu

DAY 3 · SIAM SHOPPING AND SKYLINE

  • 09:30Jim Thompson HouseGo with the guide.
  • 12:30Lunch · around Siam
  • 14:30Siam malls and MBK CenterMBK for bargaining, Siam Paragon for air-conditioned browsing.
  • 18:30Dinner · around Sukhumvit

DAY 4 · MARKETS AND SUKHUMVIT

  • 09:00Chatuchak Weekend MarketWear light clothes.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Sukhumvit
  • 15:30Thai massage in Phrom PhongA proper two-hour session resets tired feet from temple days.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Sukhumvit

DAY 5 · SLOW DEPARTURE MORNING

  • 10:00Sukhumvit → Bangkok airport (~1h)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Cover shoulders and knees at temples.
  • Use Grab or metered taxis.
  • Carry small notes for markets.
