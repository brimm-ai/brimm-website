ITINERARY · JAPAN · 3 DAYS

Kyoto in 3 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Kifune Shrine, Kyoto · yoshie yokouchi on Unsplash

Kyoto is where Japan's soul lives — ancient temples draped in moss, geisha districts where time moves slower, and bamboo groves that make you forget the modern world exists.

🌤 Best Mar–May, Oct–Nov🚇 Metro + taxi

Busy & pricey: Apr, Nov

💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND GION EVENINGS

  • 13:00Kansai airport → Kyoto (~75 min)
  • 16:00Yasaka Shrine and GionLantern-lit shrine at the edge of the geisha district.
  • 17:30Walk Hanamikoji StreetWooden teahouses line the lane.

DAY 2 · FUSHIMI INARI AND KIYOMIZU-DERA

  • 07:30Fushimi Inari TaishaThousands of vermilion gates up the hillside.
  • 11:00Kiyomizu-dera TempleWooden stage jutting over the hillside.

DAY 3 · ARASHIYAMA AND DEPARTURE

  • 08:00Arashiyama Bamboo GroveWalk the path early, the sound of bamboo is half the point.
  • 09:30Tenryu-ji Temple GardenPond garden next to the bamboo path.
  • 13:00Kyoto → Kansai airport (~75 min)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Buy an ICOCA card for buses and trains.
  • Carry cash; many small shops skip cards.
  • Remove shoes where you see a step up.
