Abu Dhabi in 3 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque · Louvre Abu Dhabi · Stroll along the Corniche — Abu Dhabi's big sights, planned day by day.

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND THE CORNICHE

  • 13:00Abu Dhabi airport → city centre (~35 min)
  • 15:00Stroll along the CornicheFlat, breezy waterfront walk with the city skyline across the bay.
  • 17:30Sunset at Emirates Palace areaThe gold-toned palace faces the water.
  • 19:30Dinner · around Corniche

DAY 2 · GRAND MOSQUE AND SAADIYAT CULTURE

  • 09:00Sheikh Zayed Grand MosqueDress modestly.
  • 12:30Lunch · around Saadiyat Island
  • 14:30Louvre Abu Dhabi Book aheadThe rain-of-light dome is the real showpiece.

DAY 3 · LAST LOOK AND DEPARTURE

  • 10:30Qasr Al WatanThe presidential palace has a grand hall worth seeing slowly.
  • 13:00Abu Dhabi city → airport (~35 min)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Dress modestly for the Grand Mosque.
  • Ride-hailing apps are cheap and reliable.
  • Carry a light layer for cold indoor air-conditioning.
