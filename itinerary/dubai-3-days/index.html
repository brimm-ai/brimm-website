Dubai in 3 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Dubai, UAE · David Rodrigo on Unsplash

Burj Khalifa At The Top · Dubai Mall and Dubai Aquarium · Dubai Creek and Al Fahidi Historic District — Dubai's big sights, planned day by day.

🌤 Best Nov–Mar🚇 Metro + taxi

Busy & pricey: Dec–Jan

💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVE AND OLD DUBAI

  • 14:00Dubai airport → Bur Dubai (~30 min)
  • 16:00Dubai Creek and Al Fahidi Historic DistrictWind towers and narrow lanes.
  • 18:30Gold and Spice SouksThe alleys are narrower and louder than you expect, and haggling is simply how everything works.
  • 20:00Dinner · around Bur Dubai

DAY 2 · DOWNTOWN ICONS

  • 09:30Burj Khalifa At The Top Book aheadGo up in morning light for the clearest views.
  • 12:30Lunch · around Downtown Dubai
  • 14:30Dubai Mall and Dubai AquariumThe aquarium tunnel is the best-value part of the mall.
  • 18:30Dinner · around Downtown Dubai

DAY 3 · MARINA MORNING AND DEPARTURE

  • 09:45Dubai Marina WalkFlat promenade, yachts on one side, towers on the other.
  • 11:00Dubai Marina → Dubai airport (~35 min)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Metro is cheap and cool; taxis for evenings.
  • Cover shoulders and knees in old Dubai mosques.
  • Burj Khalifa tickets are cheaper in advance.
