3 days in Muscat: Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque & Mutrah Souq

Muscat is the Middle East's best-kept secret — a gleaming white city wedged between dramatic mountains and a turquoise sea, where incense-scented souqs and a stunning royal opera house sit alongside…

🌤 Best Oct–Nov, Mar🚗 Mostly by car💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · MUTRAH CORNICHE WALK & ROYAL OPERA HOUSE MUSCAT

  • 14:00Muscat airport → Mutrah (~35 min)
  • 15:30Mutrah Corniche WalkHarbour, fishing boats and mountains behind.
  • 17:00Royal Opera House MuscatStriking white-stone exterior; the gardens and courtyards are lovely to wander.

DAY 2 · SULTAN QABOOS GRAND MOSQUE & MUTRAH SOUQ

  • 08:30Sultan Qaboos Grand MosqueDress modestly.
  • 11:00Mutrah SouqCovered maze of lanes.
  • 16:00Al Alam Palace and Old MuscatThe palace itself is viewed from the gate; the forts above frame the photo.

DAY 3 · EASY LAST MORNING

  • 10:30Qurum Beach StrollWide sand and calm water, good for a last unhurried walk.
  • 12:30Muscat → Muscat airport (~35 min)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Cover shoulders and knees at the Grand Mosque.
  • Taxis are plentiful; agree fares before riding.
  • Carry small notes for souq purchases.
