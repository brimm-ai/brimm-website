ITINERARY · JAPAN · 7 DAYS

7 days in Tokyo: Asakusa and Old Tokyo

Tokyo, Japan · Louie Martinez on Unsplash

Tokyo is a sensory overload in the best possible way — ancient temples tucked between neon-lit skyscrapers, and more Michelin-starred restaurants than anywhere else on earth.

🌤 Best Sep–Nov🚇 Metro + taxi💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND MARUNOUCHI EVENING

  • 14:00Tokyo airport → Tokyo Station (~1h)
  • 17:00Marunouchi Evening StrollBrick-lined avenues glow after dark.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Tokyo Station

DAY 2 · ASAKUSA AND OLD TOKYO

  • 09:00Sensoji TempleArrive early; the main hall is calmer before the crowds.
  • 11:00Nakamise Street SnacksTry freshly grilled senbei straight from the stalls.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Asakusa
  • 15:30Sumida River WalkRiverside views of Skytree; best in golden afternoon light.

DAY 3 · SHINJUKU HIGHLIGHTS

  • 09:30Tokyo Metropolitan Government BuildingFree observation decks; clear mornings sometimes show Fuji.
  • 12:30Lunch · around Shinjuku
  • 15:00Shinjuku GyoenAutumn colour peaks around here; go slowly.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Shinjuku

DAY 4 · ROPPONGI ART AND SKYLINE

  • 10:00Mori Art MuseumContemporary art with skyline views from the same floor.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Roppongi
  • 15:30Tokyo TowerClassic red tower; go near dusk for both light and lights.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Roppongi

DAY 5 · SHIBUYA AND HARAJUKU

  • 09:30Meiji ShrineForest path in the middle of the city; quiet and cool.
  • 14:30Shibuya CrossingView it from above first, then cross it yourselves.

DAY 6 · LAST FULL DAY: GINZA AND NIHONBASHI

  • 11:30Imperial Palace East GardensWide lawns and old stone walls; autumn colour is lovely.
  • 14:00Ginza Shopping StrollDepartment store basements are a food and gift paradise.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Tokyo Station

DAY 7 · DEPARTURE DAY

  • 09:30Tokyo Station → Tokyo airport (~1h)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Get a Suica card for trains.
  • Carry cash; some small places skip cards.
  • Don't eat while walking in quiet streets.
