Cairo in 5 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Giza, Egypt · Adrian Dascal on Unsplash

Cairo is where ancient wonder meets chaotic, intoxicating energy — you can watch the sun rise over the Pyramids of Giza, then lose yourself in the labyrinthine spice markets of Khan el-Khalili by afternoon.

🌤 Best Oct–Apr🚗 Mostly by car

Busy & pricey: Dec–Jan

  • 14:00Cairo airport → Zamalek (~45 min)
  • 16:00Nile Corniche StrollEasy first walk.
  • 18:00Sunset Felucca RideHaggle before boarding.

DAY 2 · PYRAMIDS OF GIZA

  • 08:00Great Pyramids of GizaGo early.
  • 11:30Sphinx and Valley TempleThe Sphinx is smaller than photos suggest.
  • 15:00Grand Egyptian Museum Book aheadRamses II greets you in the atrium.

  • 09:00Saladin CitadelHilltop fortress with Muhammad Ali Mosque.
  • 14:30Al-Muizz StreetOpen-air museum of Mamluk mosques.
  • 17:30Khan el-Khalili BazaarBargain with a smile.
