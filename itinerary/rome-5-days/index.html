ITINERARY · ITALY · 5 DAYS

Rome in 5 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Rome, Italy · Zach Dyson on Unsplash

Colosseum · Roman Forum and Palatine Hill · Trevi Fountain at dusk — Rome's big sights in the first three days, the rest your way.

🌤 Best Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct🛏 Stay: Trastevere🚇 Metro + taxi

Busy & pricey: Jul

💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND TRASTEVERE EVENING

  • 14:00Fiumicino airport → Trastevere (~45 min)
  • 16:00Check in · Trastevere
  • 17:30Wander Trastevere's lanesIvy-covered streets, small piazzas, and no need for a plan.
  • 19:30Dinner · around Trastevere

DAY 2 · ANCIENT ROME

  • 08:30Colosseum Book aheadThe arena floor view explains the whole building at once.
  • 11:00Roman Forum and Palatine Hill Book aheadWalk uphill first, the Forum looks best from above.
  • 13:30Lunch · around Monti
  • 16:00Trevi Fountain at duskIt fills a tiny square so completely that you hear the water before you see it.

DAY 3 · VATICAN CITY

  • 08:00Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel Book aheadGo straight to the Sistine Chapel, then work backwards.
  • 11:30St. Peter's BasilicaMichelangelo's Pietà sits just inside on the right.
  • 13:30Lunch · around Prati
  • 16:00Castel Sant'AngeloClimb to the terrace for the best sunset view over Rome.

DAYS 4–5

  • Day 4Piazzas, Pantheon and Spanish StepsPantheonPiazza Navona and Campo de' Fiori
  • Day 5Slow last morning and departureVilla Borghese stroll

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Book Colosseum and Vatican tickets early.
  • Cover shoulders and knees for churches.
  • Refill bottles at street nasoni fountains.
