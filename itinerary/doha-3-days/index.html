ITINERARY · QATAR · 3 DAYS

Doha in 3 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Doha, Qatar · Lucca Belliboni on Unsplash

Doha is where the ancient Gulf meets an audacious vision of the future — a skyline of gravity-defying towers rising beside a corniche where fishermen still cast at dawn.

🌤 Best Nov–Apr🚗 Mostly by car

💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND THE CORNICHE

  • 14:00Hamad airport → Doha city (~30 min)
  • 16:00Doha Corniche walkFlat, breezy waterfront.
  • 18:00Museum of Islamic Art exterior and parkThe building is the show.

DAY 2 · MUSEUMS, SOUQ AND THE PEARL

  • 09:30National Museum of QatarDesert-rose architecture inside and out.
  • 15:00Souq Waqif and falcon souqWander the lanes, then peek at the falcon shops.

DAY 3 · EASY LAST MORNING

  • 10:30Katara Cultural Village strollAmphitheatre, pigeon towers and a quiet beach, all within easy reach.
  • 13:00Doha city → Hamad airport (~30 min)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Dress modestly in souqs and museums.
  • Doha Metro is cheap and clean.
  • Karwa taxis and Uber both work well.
