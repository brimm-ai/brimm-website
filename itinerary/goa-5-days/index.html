5 days in Goa: Fort Aguada and Candolim

Goa moves at its own unhurried pace — the Portuguese call it susegado, a word meaning contentment without need to rush, and it clings to the state like saltwater and frangipani.

🌤 Best Nov, Jan–Mar🚗 Mostly by car💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND CALANGUTE SUNSET

  • 13:00Goa airport → Calangute (~1h15)
  • 16:30Calangute Beach strollWalk north towards Baga.

DAY 2 · FORT AGUADA AND CANDOLIM

  • 10:30Fort AguadaLaterite walls above the Arabian Sea.
  • 18:00Sunset at Sinquerim BeachQuieter than Baga, with the fort on the headland behind you.

DAY 3 · ANJUNA AND VAGATOR COAST

  • 11:00Chapora FortThe fort of Dil Chahta Hai fame.
  • 17:00Sunset at Vagator BeachBlack-and-red cliffs frame the sunset.

DAY 4 · FONTAINHAS AND OLD GOA HERITAGE

  • 09:30Fontainhas Latin QuarterPortuguese-painted lanes in Panjim.
  • 15:00Basilica of Bom JesusHolds the preserved remains of St Francis Xavier.

DAY 5 · SLOW MORNING AND DEPARTURE

  • 10:30Last walk on Calangute BeachEarly light and empty sand make for the best photos of the trip.
  • 11:30Pick up souvenirs in CalanguteCashews, feni and spices make easy gifts.
  • 13:00Calangute → Goa airport (~1h15)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Rent a scooter for flexible beach hopping.
  • Carry cash for beach shacks.
  • Cover shoulders and knees at churches.
