ITINERARY · SINGAPORE · 3 DAYS

Singapore in 3 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Gardens by the Bay and Supertree light show · Sentosa Island and Siloso Beach · Marina Bay Sands and the waterfront — Singapore's big sights, planned day by day.

🌤 Best Feb–Mar🚇 Metro + taxi

💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · CHINATOWN HERITAGE WALK, TEMPLE & GARDENS BY THE BAY AND SUPERTREE LIGHT SHOW

  • 14:00Changi Airport → Marina Bay (~30 min)
  • 16:30Chinatown heritage walk and templeThe Buddha Tooth Relic Temple glows best at dusk.
  • 18:30Gardens by the Bay and Supertree light showWalk the OCBC Skyway among the Supertrees at dusk.

DAY 2 · SENTOSA ISLAND, SILOSO BEACH & MARINA BAY SANDS AND THE WATERFRONT

  • 10:45Sentosa Island and Siloso BeachThe cable car from Mount Faber beats the bridge for views.
  • 15:45Lunch · around Chinatown
  • 18:30Marina Bay Sands and the waterfrontThe Promontory gives the best frontal view of the skyline.

DAY 3 · KAMPONG GELAM AND DEPARTURE

  • 10:15Sultan Mosque and Haji LaneThe golden dome rises over a lane of murals and indie shops, so wander both slowly.
  • 12:00Lunch · around Kampong Gelam
  • 14:00Kampong Gelam → Changi Airport (~35 min)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Tap your contactless card on MRT gates.
  • Dress light but carry a layer for cold malls.
  • Grab is the easiest taxi app.
