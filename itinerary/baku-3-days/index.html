3 days in Baku: Palaces, Heydar Aliyev and Flame Towers

Baku is a city where a medieval walled old town sits in the shadow of flame-shaped glass skyscrapers — an utterly surreal collision of Silk Road history and petro-state ambition.

🌤 Best Apr–May, Sep–Oct🚶 Mostly on foot💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVE AND OLD CITY EVENING

  • 15:30Icherisheher (Old City)Walled maze of lanes.
  • 18:00Seaside Boulevard strollFlat promenade; the flame towers light up as dusk falls.

DAY 2 · PALACES, HEYDAR ALIYEV AND FLAME TOWERS

  • 09:30Palace of the ShirvanshahsQuiet courtyards; start early, the tour buses come later.
  • 14:30Heydar Aliyev CenterZaha Hadid's curves look best from the lawn outside.

DAY 3 · LAST MORNING AND DEPARTURE

  • 09:45Fountains Square & Nizami StreetPedestrian street with cafés; easy to leave at any point.
  • 11:00Baku → Baku airport (~30 min)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Taxi apps beat street hails in Baku.
  • Carry some cash for small cafés.
  • Wear flat shoes for Old City cobbles.
