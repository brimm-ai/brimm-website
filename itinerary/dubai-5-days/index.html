ITINERARY · UAE · 5 DAYS

Dubai in 5 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Dubai, UAE · David Rodrigo on Unsplash

Dubai Mall and Aquarium · Burj Khalifa At The Top · Desert safari with dinner — Dubai's big sights in the first three days, the rest your way.

🌤 Best Nov–Mar🚇 Metro + taxi

Busy & pricey: Dec–Jan

💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND DUBAI CREEK EVENING

  • 15:00Dubai airport → Bur Dubai (~30 min)
  • 17:00Dubai Creek abra rideWooden boats cross the creek, pay the driver directly.
  • 18:00Old Dubai souks strollTextile and spice souks sit minutes apart on foot.
  • 20:00Dinner · around Bur Dubai

DAY 2 · BURJ KHALIFA AND DOWNTOWN

  • 11:00Dubai Mall and Aquarium Book aheadThe tunnel walk-through is the part worth the entry.
  • 14:00Lunch · around Downtown Dubai
  • 17:00Burj Khalifa At The Top Book aheadGolden hour shows both the desert and the skyline.

DAY 3 · DESERT SAFARI DAY

  • 11:00Jumeirah Mosque and BeachPhotograph the mosque, then walk the sand toward Burj Al Arab.
  • 15:30Desert safari with dinnerDune drive, camel stop and sunset over the sand.

DAYS 4–5

  • Day 4Palm Jumeirah and MarinaPalm Jumeirah monorail and viewsJBR beach time
  • Day 5Easy final morning and departureLast souvenir stop
