Tbilisi in 5 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Tbilisi, Georgia · Anna Bannahyan on Unsplash

Narikala Fortress by cable car · Abanotubani Sulphur Baths · Stroll Betlemi and Sioni Streets — Tbilisi's big sights in the first three days, the rest your way.

🌤 Best May–Jun, Sep–Oct🚶 Mostly on foot

Busy & pricey: Jul–Aug

💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND OLD TOWN STROLL

  • 14:00Tbilisi airport → Old Town (~30 min)
  • 16:30Stroll Betlemi and Sioni StreetsCarved wooden balconies lean over the lanes.
  • 18:30Evening on Rustaveli AvenueWalk the main boulevard as the facades light up.

DAY 2 · OLD TOWN ICONS AND SULPHUR BATHS

  • 09:30Narikala Fortress by cable carThe cable car beats the climb.
  • 15:00Abanotubani Sulphur BathsTake a private room.

DAY 3 · MTSKHETA AND JVARI

  • 09:30Jvari MonasteryA sixth-century church on a hilltop above two rivers meeting.
  • 11:30Svetitskhoveli CathedralDress modestly.

DAYS 4–5

  • Day 4Markets and museumsDezerter BazaarGeorgian National Museum
  • Day 5Slow morning and departure

  • Cover shoulders and knees at churches.
  • Carry some cash for markets and small cafes.
