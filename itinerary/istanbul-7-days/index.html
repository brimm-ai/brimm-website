7 days in Istanbul: Sultanahmet's Great Monuments

Istanbul, Türkiye · Engin Yapici on Unsplash

Istanbul is the only city in the world straddling two continents, where Byzantine mosaics and Ottoman domes share skylines with rooftop bars buzzing until dawn.

🌤 Best Apr–May, Sep–Oct🛏 Stay: Beyoğlu🚇 Metro + taxi💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVE AND SETTLE IN BEYOĞLU

  • 14:00Istanbul airport → Beyoğlu (~1h)
  • 17:30Galata Bridge at SunsetWalk across with the fishermen; ferries cross the Bosphorus below.
  • 19:30Dinner · around Beyoğlu

DAY 2 · SULTANAHMET'S GREAT MONUMENTS

  • 09:00Hagia SophiaLook up first, then walk the whole floor slowly.
  • 11:00Blue MosqueCarry a scarf; shoes come off at the door.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Historic Peninsula
  • 15:00Basilica CisternCool, dim and quiet; find the Medusa column bases at the back.

DAY 3 · PALACES AND THE SPICE BAZAAR

  • 09:00Topkapı PalaceThe Harem is a separate section; give it its own time.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Historic Peninsula
  • 15:00Spice BazaarTaste before you buy; lokum and spices make easy gifts.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Beyoğlu

DAY 4 · ACROSS TO THE ASIAN SIDE

  • 11:00Kadıköy Market WanderCheese, olives and pickles stalls; graze rather than sit.
  • 13:30Lunch · around Asian Side
  • 16:30Çamlıca Hill ViewpointThe widest view of the city; stay for the lights coming on.

DAY 5 · BOSPHORUS CRUISE AND GALATA

  • 10:00Bosphorus Boat CruiseSit on the upper deck for palaces and wooden mansions.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Beyoğlu
  • 15:00Galata TowerGo up for the view; Karaköy's streets below suit a wander.
  • 18:00İstiklal Caddesi StrollWalk it end to end; the red tram runs the full length.

DAY 6 · BAZAARS AND A HAMMAM

  • 10:00Grand BazaarStart with the outer streets; the real bargains lie deeper in.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Historic Peninsula
  • 15:30Traditional Turkish HammamScrub and foam massage, a couples' session works well.
  • 19:30Dinner · around Beyoğlu

DAY 7 · LAST MORNING AND DEPARTURE

  • 09:30Check out · Beyoğlu
  • 10:00Beyoğlu → Istanbul airport (~1h)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Istanbulkart works on trams, metro, buses and ferries.
  • Cover shoulders and knees at mosques.
  • Haggle in bazaars, not in shops with fixed prices.
