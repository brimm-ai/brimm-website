7 days in Bali: Ubud's Rice Terraces and Temples

Bali, Indonesia · Niklas Weiss on Unsplash

Bali seduces like nowhere else on earth — emerald rice terraces stepping down volcanic hillsides in Ubud, sunrise ceremonies at thousand-year-old temples wreathed in incense smoke, and Seminyak beach…

🌤 Best Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct🚗 Mostly by car💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND UBUD SETTLE-IN

  • 13:00Bali airport → Ubud (~1h 30)
  • 16:00Ubud Palace and Market StrollThe market is chaotic; carving and baskets are the real finds.
  • 18:30Dinner · around Ubud

DAY 2 · UBUD'S RICE TERRACES AND TEMPLES

  • 08:00Tegallalang Rice TerracesArrive early; the light is softer and the paths are quieter.
  • 12:30Lunch · around Ubud
  • 15:30Sacred Monkey Forest SanctuaryKeep sunglasses and loose items in a zipped bag.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Ubud

DAY 3 · TEMPLES, WATER AND A SLOW AFTERNOON

  • 08:30Goa Gajah Elephant CaveThe carved cave mouth is the point; the bathing pools sit behind.
  • 11:00Tirta Empul Holy Spring TempleWear a sarong, bring a change of clothes if joining the ritual.
  • 13:30Lunch · around Ubud
  • 19:00Legong Dance at Ubud PalaceSeats on the stage side show the dancers' hands best.

DAY 4 · MOVE TO SOUTH BALI

  • 13:30Lunch · around Seminyak
  • 16:00Seminyak Beach WalkThe sand is firm and flat near the waterline.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Seminyak

DAY 5 · ULUWATU CLIFFS AND SUNSET

  • 09:30Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural ParkThe statue is huge.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Uluwatu
  • 16:30Uluwatu Temple Sunset and Kecak DanceHold on to glasses and phones; the macaques steal them.

DAY 6 · BEACH DAY AND LAST EVENING

  • 11:00Spa TreatmentA Balinese massage fits this day.
  • 14:00Canggu Beach StopBlack-sand surf beach, popular with locals at sundown.
  • 19:00Dinner · around Seminyak

DAY 7 · DEPARTURE DAY

  • 10:00Seminyak Village ShoppingSmall boutiques selling linen and silver.
  • 12:00Seminyak → Bali airport (~45 min)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Wear a sarong into temples.
  • Use Grab or Gojek for easy rides.
  • Carry small cash for entry fees.
