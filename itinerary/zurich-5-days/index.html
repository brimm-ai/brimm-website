Zurich in 5 days: a day-by-day itinerary

The food scene punches well above its size, and the Alps are just an hour away for spontaneous day trips.

  • 14:00Zurich airport → city centre (~15 min)

  • 17:30Lake Zurich Promenade at SunsetWalk the quay and watch the swans near the Opera House.
