ITINERARY · THAILAND · 3 DAYS

Chiang Mai in 3 days: a day-by-day itinerary

Chiang Mai, Thailand · matt mu on Unsplash

Chiang Mai is where ancient temple bells echo through misty mountains and the smell of lemongrass drifts from a thousand street stalls.

🌤 Best Nov–Jan🚇 Metro + taxi

Busy & pricey: Nov–Jan

💰 Costs in your currency, in the app

DAY 1 · ARRIVAL AND OLD CITY EVENING

  • 12:00Chiang Mai airport → Old City (~20 min)
  • 16:00Wat Chedi LuangA ruined brick chedi, once among the tallest in the north.

DAY 2 · DOI SUTHEP AND OLD CITY TEMPLES

  • 08:00Doi Suthep temple309 steps up a naga staircase.
  • 15:00Wat Phra SinghHome to the Lion Buddha.

DAY 3 · SLOW LAST MORNING AND DEPARTURE

  • 10:00Warorot MarketA busy local market for northern snacks and sweets.
  • 12:00Old City → Chiang Mai airport (~20 min)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Grab app works well for taxis in Chiang Mai.
  • Cover shoulders and knees at temples.
  • Carry small notes for temple entry fees.
