DAY 1 · ARRIVAL & SEINE STROLL

  • 14:00Paris airport → city centre (~1h)
  • 16:00Walk Île de la Cité & Notre-Dame exteriorThe cathedral's facade is best seen from the square.
  • 17:45Stroll the Seine quaysFollow the river toward Pont Neuf.
  • 19:30Dinner · around Latin Quarter

DAY 2 · EIFFEL TOWER & INVALIDES

  • 09:00Eiffel Tower Book aheadWalk up to the second floor, the lifts do the rest.
  • 12:30Lunch · around Eiffel Tower
  • 14:30Les Invalides & Napoleon's TombThe golden dome is visible from half the city.
  • 19:30Dinner · around Saint Germain

DAY 3 · THE LOUVRE & TUILERIES

  • 09:00Louvre Museum Book aheadPick three wings, not the whole building.
  • 13:00Lunch · around Louvre Chatelet

  • 19:00Seine evening cruiseThe bridges and monuments are all lit after dark.
