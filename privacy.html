Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

brimm is run by Brimm Limited, United Arab Emirates. Under data protection law, Brimm Limited is the "data controller" for your personal information. You can reach us at support@brimm.ai for any privacy question or request.

2. Information we collect

We collect what we need to plan your trips and check your entry requirements.

When you sign up and set up your profile

  • Your email address. We sign you in with a one-time code sent to your email. There is no password.
  • If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address and your name. We never see your Google password.
  • Your name.
  • Your passport country, and if you add it, your passport's expiry date, so we can check it meets each destination's rule.
  • Visas and residence permits you hold (for example a US or Schengen visa), with their expiry dates, for a residence permit the country that issued it, and — if you tell us — whether a visa is single- or multiple-entry.
  • Your home country, your phone's country (never your phone number), and your home city if you choose one ("Flying from?"), used to start flight searches from your nearest airport. You can change or clear it in Profile.
  • Your travel preferences, such as spend level, interests and currency.
  • A profile photo, if you add one. The App asks for your camera or photos only when you choose one. It is stored with your account and anyone who has its web address can open it, so please don't use a photo you would not want others to see. Nobody can browse a list of photos, and only you can change or remove yours.

Your location — only a city, never your position

  • Your travel map. The countries and cities you add, the month and year when known, and whether brimm saw you there (brimmed ✓) are stored with your account so your map follows you. You can remove any place.
  • "📍 I'm here". When you tap it, the App reads your phone's approximate position once, on the phone, to find the nearest city in its own list. The position never leaves your phone and is never stored — only the city, its country and the month are kept.
  • "Starting from" on a day trip. If you plan a day trip and set where you start, brimm sends only the name of the area (for example a neighbourhood or a hotel name) to plan the way there and back — never your GPS position. If you allow location, the area is found from your phone's position; otherwise you type it.
  • brimm never asks for precise location and never reads your location in the background. You can turn location off for brimm in your phone's settings; everything else keeps working.

As you use the App

  • Trips and itineraries you create or save, and anything you add to them: arrival and departure times, where you are staying, prices you type in, places you add, stops you star, and the message and highlights you pick for a share card.
  • Places you save (your brimmings) and places you recently viewed.
  • A consent record: the date and version of the terms you agreed to.

Feedback you send us

  • In Profile → Feedback, we keep what you choose to send: the type, your message and up to 3 screenshots or photos, with the App's version and your device model so we can find the problem. It is stored with your account.
  • Your pictures are kept in private storage that only we can open, with a small, lower-quality copy of each next to your message.
  • A screenshot shows whatever was on your screen, so check it before you send it.

Collected automatically

  • Your sign-in session, so the App knows it is you.
  • How you use the App. We record a small number of in-app actions, linked to your account, to see which features help travellers and to fix what doesn't. For example: opening the App; creating a trip (the destination, number of nights, spend level and number of travellers); opening a destination (the city); sharing a trip, a card or the PDF; opening a map or a booking link; setting an arrival or departure time (the time you set); using brimm AI (counts only); and whether "I'm here" worked (the kind of answer only — never a place). We never record the words you type into the trip chat or brimm AI as an event.
  • Voice, only if you use it. If you tap 🎙, your phone's own speech service turns what you say into words in the box. brimm receives only the words, never a recording. The speech service may process your voice under its provider's own terms. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.
  • Your plan's allowances. Our server counts each trip, rebuild and AI message against your account so it can apply your plan.
  • An anonymous count of searched destinations. Opening a destination from search adds one to that day's count for that place, with no account, no device and no time of day on it. It powers "Popular picks". The words you type in search are never stored.
  • Crash reports. If the App crashes or hits an error, a report goes to our crash-reporting service: your device model, operating system, App version, the error and the steps just before it. It does not include your name, email, passport details or trips, and the App tells the provider not to record your internet (IP) address.
  • App update checks. When the App starts, it asks our app-update service whether a newer version is ready. The request carries the kind of phone (Android or iPhone), the App's version and update channel, the update it is running, and a random number the App makes on your phone the first time it runs, so the service can tell installs apart. None of it is linked to your name or account.
  • Visa checks without an account. If you check a visa without an account, we keep a random code from your phone so we can count your free checks. It isn't linked to your name and is deleted after 12 months.
  • Missing-photo reports. When a place in your trip shows no photo, the App sends us the place's name, its city and what went wrong — without your account or your trip — so we can find better photos. We keep these reports for 90 days.

What we do not collect: your precise location, any location in the background, contacts, calendar, text messages or payment card details. There are no advertising trackers in the App.

brimm Pro and payments

  • We never see or store your card details. When brimm Pro is on sale, you buy it through the App Store or Google Play, under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • The store tells us, through our subscription provider, whether your subscription is active and when it ends, linked to your brimm account so your plan follows you.
  • For our accounts and tax, we keep a record of each purchase: the date, the store, the country, the currency and price, tax, the store's fee, until when Pro is paid for, and the store's transaction numbers. It never holds card details (§6 says what happens to it when you delete your account).
  • There are no free trials that turn into payments. You can cancel any time in the App Store or Google Play.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • write your itineraries and answer your questions in the trip chat and brimm AI;
  • check entry requirements for your passport and the documents you hold — the heart of brimm;
  • keep your travel map, and back up your trips, places and profile so they are there on a new phone;
  • apply your plan (Free or Pro) — a request that fails on our side is not counted;
  • reply to your feedback and support requests, and fix what you report;
  • understand which features help travellers, and fix faults;
  • count totals across all travellers — for example how many trips were planned this week, or which destinations are planned most — to see how brimm is doing and to improve it. These totals never name anyone, and a group of fewer than 5 travellers is never shown;
  • keep a record that you agreed to our Terms of Service and this policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads, and we do not use your data for advertising.

So a trip is never lost. When we plan a trip, our server keeps a copy of the finished plan for 24 hours. If your phone loses the connection while it waits, the App fetches that copy when you come back, and you are not charged twice.

Trip reminders. With your permission, brimm reminds you about your own trips — a countdown, your Day 1 plan, a passport that needs renewing, "you're in …" to stamp a city, and when you are close to your next level. They are scheduled on your phone from your saved trips; nothing about them is sent to us or anyone else, and they are never ads. Choose which you get in Profile → Notifications, or turn them all off in your phone's settings.

Planning in the background (Android). While a trip is being planned, brimm keeps working if you switch apps and shows a "planning your trip" notice. It stops when the trip is ready, and after 5 minutes at most. It does not use your location and sends nothing of its own.

Emails from us. Today the only email brimm sends you is your sign-in code. No newsletters, no marketing.

When you share a trip. Share cards, story images, your travel map card and your trip PDF are made on your phone and go wherever you send them through your phone's share options; brimm does not send them anywhere itself. A card can show your destination, the entry result for your passport and, only if you turn them on, your dates, costs and number of travellers — so check it before you send it.

4. Who we work with

We use a few service providers to run brimm. Each one gets only what its job needs, and handles it only for us. A list of our service providers is available on request at support@brimm.ai.

Our hosting and database provider — stores your account, profile, trips, travel map, places, usage events, feedback and photos. Hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

An AI service that creates your itinerary and answers questions — it writes your itineraries and answers. The App never talks to it directly: every request goes through brimm's own server, which holds the keys. Our server sends the details each answer needs:

  • making or rebuilding a trip: your destinations, dates and nights, who is travelling (for example "family with kids"), spend level, interests, anything you typed about the trip, arrival and departure times, where you are staying, a day trip's starting area if you set one, your passport country, the visas you hold, and brimm's entry result for each city;
  • the trip chat (including "Add food stops"): what you type, the stops and times in your plan, your interests, your passport country, the visas and residence permits you hold with their expiry dates, and brimm's entry results for the trip;
  • brimm AI ("Where should I go?"): what you type and your answers, your passport country, the visas and residence permits you hold, and the country you live in, and, if you set one, your home city's name (to say roughly how far each place is).

We never send your name, email address or account details to the AI service. Your chat conversations stay on your phone; we do not store them on our servers (but see §6 for messages the chat declines). The AI service's terms say it does not train its models on what we send, and deletes it within 30 days; a request flagged as breaking its usage policy may be kept for up to 2 years.

A visa-information provider — to check entry rules, our server sends it two things only: your passport country and the destination country, as two-letter codes. It never receives your name, account or the visas you hold — brimm applies those itself. Answers are stored by country pair, with no account on them.

A photo library — destination photos come from a photo library. Our server searches it with place names, never your name or account. Your phone then loads each photo straight from the library, so the library sees your device's internet address, as any website would. When you share a card with a photo, we tell the library which photo was used, as it requires — that message carries the photo, not you.

Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm who you are. Google's privacy policy applies to that sign-in.

Subscription provider — when brimm Pro is on sale, it tells us whether your subscription is active and when it ends (§2). It receives your brimm account id and the store's purchase details, never your card.

A crash-reporting service — receives the crash reports in §2. Hosted in the European Union (Germany).

A map provider — draws the trip map and the travel globe. The App asks it for map images of the area on screen; it never receives your account, trips or passport details. It counts how many phones use the map each month with an anonymous ID, so it can bill us, and its own usage reporting is switched off.

App-update service — tells the App when a newer version is ready (§2).

Feedback — stored with our hosting and database provider. The brimm team reads it, helped by an AI assistant from the AI service above, which we use to sort and summarise messages and draft replies. It sees your message, its type, the App version, your device model and the small copies of your pictures — not your email address. A person decides every reply.

Booking and travel partners — some buttons in brimm open a travel partner's website, for tours, eSIMs, car hire, flights or hotels. We may earn a small commission if you book. Your price is the same. brimm has no ads. A link opens only when you tap it, and it may carry the place, your trip dates and the number of travellers, and for flights the airports you fly from and to — never your name or account. Once you are on a partner's site, its own privacy policy applies. The same is true when you open a stop or a day in a maps app.

5. How your information is stored and protected

Your account data is stored by our hosting and database provider, on servers in the European Union. All of it travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. API keys are kept on our servers and never in the App.

Some of your data is also kept on your phone so the App is quick and works with a weak signal. A few things stay only on your phone: your trip chat conversations, which tips you have seen, your vibration setting, your reminder settings and recently opened destinations. Signing out keeps this copy on the phone, so your trips are there when you sign back in. It is removed when you delete your account, when a different account signs in on the same phone, or when you delete the App.

Our own work board. brimm keeps its internal to-do lists in a separate, walled-off part of the database. The App cannot reach it, and it holds no trips, profiles or passport details. The only thing about travellers on it is a short summary of each feedback message, written without your name or email, so we can sort and answer it. It is deleted with your account.

No way of storing or sending data is completely secure, but we take care to protect it.

6. How long we keep your information, and deleting it

We keep your information while your account exists.

Deleting your account deletes everything linked to it, straight away: your profile (including your home city), trips and itineraries, travel map, visa details, saved and recent places, usage events, feedback and its pictures, AI usage counts, chat records, plan records, any 24-hour trip copy (§3), the feedback summaries on our work board (§5), and your profile photo. Do it in the App (Profile → Delete account) or at brimm.ai/delete-account.html.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first, or the store keeps charging you.

Empty logins. If you start "Continue with Google" and stop before finishing sign-up, the empty login it leaves (no passport, no terms agreed, no data) is removed after about a week.

What account deletion cannot reach:

  • Purchase records (§2), which we keep for our accounts and tax. When you delete your account, your account is removed from them, so they no longer point to you. We keep them for as long as UAE tax law requires: seven years after the end of the tax year.
  • Crash reports already sent. They carry no name or account, and the provider deletes them after 30 days.
  • The anonymous search counts, visa checks made without an account and missing-photo reports (§2). None has an account on it; they are deleted after 12 months (visa checks) and 90 days (photo reports).
  • What the AI service keeps under its own terms: up to 30 days, or up to 2 years for a flagged request (§4).
  • Records we must keep by law, such as your consent record.

Messages the trip chat couldn't help with. When you ask the trip chat something outside travel planning and it declines, we may keep the words of that one message for up to 30 days, to learn what travellers want from brimm. It is kept without your name, email address or account. Before it is saved we remove email addresses, phone numbers, passport and booking numbers, and names we can spot — but we can miss a name on its own, so please don't put personal details in chat messages. After 30 days it is deleted automatically.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can:

  • see a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete your account and data;
  • object to or limit how we use it;
  • take it with you in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent you gave, at any time — for example, turn off location or notifications for brimm in your phone's settings.

Much of this you can do in the App (edit your profile, remove places from your map, or delete your account). For anything else, email support@brimm.ai and we will reply within a reasonable time and as the law requires.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you live in California, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including to know what we collect and to ask us to delete it. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.

8. Where your data is processed

Your account, trips and crash reports are stored in the European Union (Germany). The AI service is a US company; its terms include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and a UK addendum for data that leaves the EU. The AI service keeps requests in the United States and may route them through its servers in the US, Europe, Asia and Australia. The app-update service is a US company that takes part in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our other providers may process data in other countries, under their own safeguards.

9. Children

brimm is not for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Adults can plan trips that include children: then brimm only knows that children are travelling and anything you choose to type about them. We do not ask for their names or ages. If you think a child has given us personal information, email support@brimm.ai and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

When brimm starts to use your information in a new way, we update this policy before that feature goes live. When we make a material change, we update the date at the top and, where appropriate, ask you to accept the new version in the App. If you keep using brimm after an update, you accept the revised policy.

Questions, concerns or requests about this policy or your data: support@brimm.ai
