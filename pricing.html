Pricing

brimm is an AI trip planner app. It checks the visa for your passport, where you live and the visas you hold, then plans each day of your trip with rough costs in your currency. It has two plans: you start on Free, with no card; brimm Pro is for when you plan more.

Free

Free to start. No card.

  • 1 trip plan, visa-checked, and 1 rebuild
  • 10 AI messages on that trip
  • 10 messages to brimm AI
  • Always free: visa checks, booking links, the share card, editing your trip

brimm Pro

Monthly or yearly

  • No counters in your way — fair use applies
  • Start a new chat whenever you like
  • A plain purchase — no free trial that turns into a charge, easy to cancel

What Pro costs

In the app

Your price shows in your local currency in the app, set by Google Play or the App Store, before you pay. You see the monthly and the yearly price side by side and choose one.

How you get Pro

brimm Pro is a digital subscription. It switches on in your brimm account as soon as the payment goes through. Nothing is shipped.

Renewal and cancelling

Pro renews automatically at the end of each month or year until you cancel. You can cancel at any time; you keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. How to cancel, and how refunds work, is on our Refunds & cancellation page.
