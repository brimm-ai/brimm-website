Refunds & cancellation

Draft, not published · [FOUNDER: date it goes live]

This page explains how to cancel brimm Pro and how to ask for a refund. Who refunds you depends on where you paid.

1. The Free plan

The Free plan never charges you. There is nothing to cancel.

2. Pro bought in the app (Google Play or the App Store)

Google Play or the App Store takes the payment, and refunds for these purchases follow the store's own rules. To ask for one:

  • Google Play: ask for a refund from your Google Play order history, or follow Google Play's help page Request a refund on Google Play.
  • App Store: ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Cancelling a store subscription

  • Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel brimm Pro.
  • iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel brimm Pro.

When you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you have already paid for, and it does not renew.

Deleting your brimm account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store first, or you may continue to be charged.
