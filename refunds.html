Refunds & cancellation

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This page explains how to cancel brimm Pro and how to ask for a refund. Who handles your refund depends on where you bought Pro.

1. Pro bought in the iPhone app

Apple takes the payment for purchases in the iPhone app, and refunds for them are handled by Apple under its own rules. To ask for a refund, go to Apple's Report a Problem page.

2. Pro bought in the Android app through Google Play

Google Play takes the payment for purchases in the Android app, and refunds for them are handled by Google Play under its own rules. To ask for a refund, follow Google's help page Request a refund on Google Play.

3. How to cancel

  • iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel brimm Pro.
  • Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel brimm Pro.

When you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you have already paid for. Deleting your brimm account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it first, or you may continue to be charged.

4. Contact

Questions about a payment, a refund or cancelling: support@brimm.ai. brimm is run by Brimm Limited, United Arab Emirates.
